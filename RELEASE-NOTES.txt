Phoenix5200 v0.30 — separate left/right return animations

The four small-bird leftward-return poses now face left. They are horizontal
reflections of the supplied right-facing frames, with their existing phases
and drawing anchors retained. Rightward returns and every other sprite pose
are unchanged. This fixes northwest movement displaying a northeast pose.

No changes to movement, bird counts, difficulty, collision rules, sound or
display interrupts. The v0.29 large-bird scanline corrections are retained.

NTSC Atari 5200; CAR type 73; 256 KiB payload; six large birds;
four mothership escorts.
