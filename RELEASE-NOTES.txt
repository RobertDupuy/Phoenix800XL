Phoenix v0.13 — presentation and mothership-escort fixes
Atari 5200 NTSC / 16 KiB RAM / 256 KiB cartridge / CAR type 73

PHOENIX now uses 69 copies of gameplay flying pose 80. All presentation text is
rebuilt from native HUD glyphs: copyright, INSERT 1 COIN, both player/coin lines,
and the score table. There are no stray marks between N, I and X.

The corruption was caused by late display-interrupt font switches damaging the
first two scanlines of character rows. Each page now retains a single font.
Startup also bypasses the gameplay palette override. The obsolete converted
picture-page binaries are removed.

Mothership escorts now receive bullet collision checks. Birds in front of the
mothership absorb matching shots before its armor. Hit effects and awards finish
before two replacements appear. Unobstructed shots still damage the mothership.

VERIFIED ON THIS CARTRIDGE
Four Altirra presentation captures: 245,760 pixels checked, zero missing/stray
pixels and zero inconsistent color pixels. Both pages repeat and start gameplay.
Both escort slots, misses, hidden-bird phases, scoring, armor priority and
replenishment passed CPU regression fixtures. 411 campaign checks passed.
A physical-controller Altirra run shot four observed mothership escorts,
cleared the mothership and entered round two. No game-memory writes or skips.

CONTROLS
Joystick left/right. Lower fire starts/shoots; release between shots. Upper fire
activates the shield. Test keyboard: Left/Right, Left Ctrl, Left Shift.

Bird waves render at about 30 updates/s, the mothership around 15–20. Input and
audio run every NTSC VBI. Colors/audio approximate arcade hardware. Tested in
AltirraSDL with the original Atari BIOS, not yet on a physical 5200.
