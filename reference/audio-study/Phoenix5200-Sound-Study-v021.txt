PHOENIX 5200 SOUND STUDY — VERSION 0.21
2026-11-06

Result
The port is closer to the arcade in pitch resolution, firing/death behavior,
sound triggers and uninterrupted timing. The largest remaining timbre mismatch
is the large-bird siren: POKEY still produces a square wave, while the arcade
filters that waveform. High whistles remain where the arcade uses them.
Four mothership escorts are retained.

What was evaluated
Twelve categories were compared using identical sound-control events: opening
music, victory music, eight/two small birds, large birds, body and wing hits,
firing, death, shield, mothership arrival and armor hits. Separate executable
ROM tests check that gameplay actually triggers the shield, bonus-life, arrival
and impact paths. Both all-three-tune sequences and their note envelopes were
checked over 4,200 sound ticks.

The reference audio comes from the checked-in MAME-derived C circuit/TMS model,
checked against current MAME source. It models the 372 Hz music chip, its 0.21 s
note timing and 0.50/1.05 s envelopes, the 555/RC effect circuits, and custom
noise. This is a reconstructed arcade reference, not a recording of a cabinet
or a new MAME gameplay session.

The before and after POKEY register streams execute the actual built 6502
sound routines. AltirraSDL renders those streams to WAV through its POKEY
emulation. The test cart is only an idle sound bench. No equalization or volume
normalization was applied to the recordings. Metrics remove DC/below-audible
capacitor offsets with a 40 Hz high-pass filter for analysis only.

Findings and implemented changes
1. Small-bird pitch steps: the old 64 kHz clock made high whistles jump between
   widely spaced pitches. Channel 3 now uses its independent 1.79 MHz clock
   where the divisor fits. In 132 such cases, maximum frequency error falls
   from 13.28% to 0.49%. Lower tones retain the normal clock.
   The old B lookup also wrapped at extreme capacitor values; that is now
   bounded. Those extreme values are outside ordinary play and were not the
   demonstrated cause of the audible squeakiness.

2. Firing: the old triggered, falling chirp did not follow the circuit. The
   new envelope rises while the fire latch is held, then releases rapidly.
   Its rate and noise clock are derived from the C24 network (0.136 s rising-noise
   envelope and 0.009044 s release). It now has the rising noisy character
   expected from the reference instead of the previous low, fading burst.

3. Death/explosion: the old noise faded out before the death signal ended.
   The replacement has a quick attack, sustained low noise while the latch
   remains active, and a separate release tail based on C25 (0.3196 s).
   The reference's roughly 400 Hz sampled-noise component is approximated with
   POKEY noise at that clock rate. Its measured brightness is now close to
   the reference; this does not mean the two noise generators are identical.

4. Large-bird siren: volume now varies with pitch according to the original
   10k || 100k / 0.047 uF filter's fundamental response. The lower part of its
   sweep is fuller relative to the high part. In the diagnostic clip, energy
   above 2 kHz falls from 9.39% to about 8.2%; the reference is only 0.4%.
   This is a modest improvement, not a reproduction of the analog filter.

5. Missing or wrong cues: shield activation now starts the original 64-tick
   control sequence; awarding an extra life starts its 255-tick pulse pattern.
   The mothership has its early arrival sound, and armor damage uses the short
   impact pattern instead of the bird-kill chime. Existing bird body/wing hit
   cues, opening music and victory music are retained.

6. Music: the recovered note sequences and basic envelope durations were
   already correct and were preserved. The port uses two musical voices;
   overlapping decay voices from the arcade chip are still simplified. Effects
   and music share four POKEY channels, so some simultaneous effect combinations
   still use priority rather than the arcade's independent generators.

7. Mothership sound timing: a three-frame queue could empty while graphics
   were being prepared. Before this fix, staged 600-frame runs missed 115 and
   49 sound updates. The mothership now queues six frames in an eight-slot ring;
   bird waves retain three. Both final runs delivered 600/600 updates with no
   gaps, no late VBI and no graphics allocation faults. Worst tested audio_tick
   cost is 673 CPU cycles; VBI publication is 128 cycles.
   The larger mothership buffer trades a little additional effect latency for
   continuous playback. Graphics still update less often than sound/simulation.

spectral balance; envelope timing, note correctness and listening preference
cannot be reduced to this number. Values are from the complete diagnostic clips.

Other changes in this build
Gameplay stars are dark blue. Small-wave red effect/shield accents share pink
to free that color; player colors, large-bird palettes and title art remain.
The mothership's initial descent finishes before the player appears or can
move, shoot or activate the shield. Four escorts remain; fast mode begins with
fewer than three survivors. The cartridge is still 256 KiB.

Validation and limits
The final ROM passed 1,744 pitch cases, 4,200 tune ticks, 512 arrival-control
cases, shield/bonus triggers, all eight ring slots and both refill depths.
Altirra checked 119 published entry frames and two 600-frame mothership loads.
Existing respawn, safe-return, shield, escort-hit, renderer and campaign checks
also passed. No physical 5200 test or new controller-only full-game completion
was performed. POKEY's square-wave harmonics, 17-bit noise and four-channel
limit remain different from the arcade hardware.

Evidence
The source archive contains evidence/current/audio with twelve reference,
previous and updated WAV clips, plus sound-study.json with all measurements.
Isolated shield/arrival/armor clips deliberately feed the same control bytes
to both drivers; they compare tone generation, not whether the old game
actually triggered those sounds. Gameplay-trigger tests are separate.
The six-escort rejection and pre-buffer measurements are separate trials with
their own ROM hashes, not results for the released cartridge.
