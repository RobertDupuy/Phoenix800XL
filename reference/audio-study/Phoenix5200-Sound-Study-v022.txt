PHOENIX 5200 — SOUND CORRECTIONS, v0.22
2026-11-06 UTC

The v0.21 sound study missed real gameplay errors. Its isolated circuit tests
could pass while the gameplay dispatcher supplied the wrong control values.
This update fixes those errors and checks the running game with native audio.

CORRECTIONS

Large-bird pulse: the old 6502 translation tested the zero flag after INC.
The arcade routine $3AD0 tests the previous counter value. The pulse therefore
never triggered in v0.21. Correcting that test restores the alternating
control stages and capacitor pulse. All 32 height-dependent cadence entries
were checked against the recovered dispatcher over 8,192 control ticks.

Wrong diving sound: the arcade $3A98 checks the animation shape byte against
$28. The port mistakenly checked Y instead. Formation shapes $20..$27 now
remain quiet at every height; active diving shapes provide the effect.

Wave-clear hang: large-bird sound continued unconditionally after the last
bird. Background bird controls now stop on clear waits, wipes and entrances;
finishing hit/noise tails still decay. Stage changes get six buffered frames.
The old live run missed four audio updates at stage changes. The new run
missed none. A fixed-bank interrupt fallback also fades the last volumes if
the foreground ever fails to refill, rather than holding a tone forever.

Cutoffs while firing: gun/death noise previously replaced the entire B
oscillator voice. It now borrows an idle musical/effect channel. This keeps
the large-bird pulse audible during firing. POKEY still has four channels;
when all voices are busy, priority remains necessary.

Mothership arrival: the small-bird clock does not advance during descent.
The arrival modulation now uses the descent countdown and stops at combat.
Its square-wave voice is reduced to volume 3/15 and transposed two octaves
lower (tested peak fundamental about 1.60 kHz). The quieter/lower entry is
an intentional adjustment for POKEY, not a claim of identical analog timbre.

Shield: reset invalidates the reused metadata and restores both buffers'
shield glyphs. A stale nonzero mothership cache byte can no longer be treated
as proof that shield artwork is loaded. The original four silhouettes and
32-row height are preserved. The initial v0.21 activation tested round; its
intermittent square appearance was not reproduced in that initial capture.
The new reset invariant is tested with overwritten glyphs and stale cache
values, and the new build's four poses were verified on the emulator screen.

ACTUAL PLAYBACK CHECK

AltirraSDL, NTSC Atari 5200, original BIOS, normal emulated hardware timing.
Controller events only; no stage injection, invulnerability or loaded states.
The native WAV duration matched the emulated clock duration.

                             v0.21 before       v0.22 after
Recorded gameplay               237.91 s           238.54 s
Missing sound updates                  4                  0
Large-bird background controls        32        32/36/48/52
Late video interrupts                  0                  0
Graphics allocation faults             0                  0

The after run observed 407 quiet formation samples and 50 cleared-wave
samples with bird sound off. There were no queue underruns after active play
started. One empty queue at initial silent startup used the silent fallback.

Both runs played the first five stages. The before run eventually completed
a cycle; the after run lost its remaining lives during mothership combat,
restarted and reached wave three. It is not a successful v0.22 completion test.

Additional executable-ROM checks: 4,200 music ticks, 1,744 pitch cases,
8,192 large-bird control ticks, 2,048 quiet formation cases, simultaneous
noise/bird output, all ring slots, fallback operation in all eight banks,
and 168 glyph-reset checks. The unchanged entrance/player gate and palettes
also passed. The shield compositor passed 160 crowded scenes; all four
controller-driven shield captures matched the original occupied silhouette
with zero pixel errors in the clean capture test.

FILES AND LIMITS

Source package evidence/current/ contains reports, controller inputs, POKEY
register traces, actual screenshots, and unprocessed native WAV excerpts:
large-bird-wave.wav and mothership-entry.wav in before/ and after/.
The after folder also includes large-wave-clear.wav. No EQ, normalization
or replacement audio was applied. The full recordings were used for analysis;
compact excerpts are distributed to avoid inflating the source package.

The recovered arcade dispatcher/reference circuit source is checked in under
reference/c/. Primary MAME sound implementation:
https://github.com/mamedev/mame/blob/master/src/mame/phoenix/phoenix_a.cpp
https://github.com/mamedev/mame/blob/master/src/devices/sound/tms36xx.cpp

This is still a POKEY approximation of separate arcade analog circuits and
music hardware. No physical Atari 5200 test was performed. Four escorts and
the existing 256 KiB cartridge format are unchanged.
