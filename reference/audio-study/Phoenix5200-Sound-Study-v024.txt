PHOENIX 5200 — PLAYER-SHIP EXPLOSION DURATION, v0.24
2026-11-06 UTC

The primary error was event duration. The player-ship explosion used the mothership's 64-tick sound pulse. Original ROM routine $0CD0 starts the player's pulse at 16; $27E2 clamps the mothership's separate trigger to 64.

Three actual MAME 0.264 gameplay deaths produced 16 explosion-control writes each, held for 0.262161091, 0.262158182 and 0.262160728 seconds. All 14 original ROM/PROM SHA-1 values matched MAME. Inputs used only coin, start, movement, fire and shield; the trace observer did not modify CPU memory. Raw, unprocessed stereo crops, sound-write timestamps, input script and ROM hashes are in evidence/current/arcade.

The released change assigns 16 ticks at damage_player and 64 at the existing mothership collision entry. The dispatcher consumes that event-specific duration. Noise modes, pitch, maximum volume, fade tables and music remain unchanged. Experimental overlap mixing is not in this release.

A 24-candidate native Altirra POKEY sweep compared four noise modes and six dividers against the three recorded arcade deaths. The existing $80 noise mode with divider 159 had the closest frequency-band match among the sweep candidates. A silent candidate was rejected. This is a statistical comparison of independent noise realizations, not waveform identity.

With the correct duration, normalized 50 ms envelope RMSE over 1.5 seconds fell from 0.585 to 0.051. These are envelope-error metrics, not percentages of perceived fidelity. The four-bit POKEY volume still has steps and its quietest tail ends earlier than the arcade. Gameplay can include a shot overlapping the explosion tail; that existing mixing behavior is unchanged here.
